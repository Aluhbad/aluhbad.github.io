Directive Seven Rulebook

Section 1: General Server Rules

Community Standards

  1. 1.1 Treat all members with courtesy and respect at all times.
  2. 1.2 Maintain a safe environment - no NSFW, discriminatory, or toxic content.
  3. 1.3 Personal information must never be shared without consent.

Communication Guidelines

  1. 1.4 Use appropriate channels for specific content (links in designated areas only).
  2. 1.5 Avoid unnecessary mentions, pings, and chat spam.
  3. 1.6 Mic spam is permitted unless someone requests you to stop.
  4. 1.7 Offensive language is strictly prohibited.

Server Integrity

  1. 1.8 Advertising, promotion, and real money transactions outside the official store are banned.
  2. 1.9 Malicious scripts, client modifications, and staff impersonation are forbidden.
  3. 1.10 Creating alternate accounts to bypass bans is prohibited.
  4. 1.11 Exploiting rulebook loopholes is forbidden - report any issues immediately.

Staff Authority

  1. 1.12 Staff meetings (sits) are mandatory - leaving during one is prohibited.
  2. 1.13 Staff decisions are final. Appeals must be submitted through Discord if you believe a punishment was unwarranted.

Section 2: Roleplay Guidelines

Core Roleplay Principles

  1. 2.1 Erotic Roleplay (ERP) is absolutely forbidden.
  2. 2.2 Respect all ongoing roleplay, including during downtime periods.
  3. 2.3 Maintain high roleplay standards - all RP must be taken seriously and fully acted out.

Character Creation

  1. 2.4 Use roleplay-appropriate names that aren't based on major Star Wars characters.

Combat and Death Rules

  1. 2.5 Player killing requires valid roleplay justification. Crossfire and accidents don't count as justification. RDM (Random Deathmatch) incidents are handled in-character.
  2. 2.6 New Life Rule (NLR): Wait 30 seconds after death before returning to the area. Neutral worlds are exempt from this timer.
  3. 2.7 FearRP: When surrounded by 3+ hostile players while under 500 HP, you must surrender. Faction Leaders are exempt from this rule.

Fair Play Standards

  1. 2.8 Metagaming - Using information from past lives or out-of-character communications is prohibited.
  2. 2.9 Powergaming - You cannot prioritize your character's power over natural roleplay flow (e.g., claiming immunity to FearRP because "my character wouldn't be scared").
  3. 2.10 Scamming other players is a bannable offense.

Structural Rules

  1. 2.11 Faction vaults cannot be breached without an active raid in progress.

Section 3: Faction Regulations

  1. 3.1 Leadership Authority - Faction leaders manage internal rules.
  2. 3.2 Council Limits - Can only be a part of one council at a time.
  3. 3.3 Enemy Spawns - Off-limits except for OOC purposes.
  4. 3.4 Safe Zones - Don't enter non-combat areas (community centers) during fights.
  5. 3.5 Organization Storage - Maximum of 20 perma-props in an organization's bunks.
  6. 3.6 Head Sitting - Prohibited in combat or when requested to stop.
  7. 3.7 Rank Changes - No mass promotions, all promotions must be at least a day apart. The player must be in-game to promote/demote.
  8. 3.8 Faction Conversion - No forced in-character conversions via mind tricks. After converting, wait one week before re-converting the same player. Converted players must be demoted to the factions lowest rank.

Sub-Section 3a: Sith Guidelines

  • 3a.1 Grace Period: The Emperor has a one week grace period after becoming the Emperor.
  • 3a.2 Overthrow:
    • The Emperor may be overthrown by any Darth+ by saying “Overthrow” in advert, causing them both to be open to Hard PK.
    • The Emperor and the initiator must be in their homeworld to call an Overthrow.
    • Any rank of sith may choose to assist or defend by saying “assist” or “defend” in advert. Any sith who calls assist or defend are open to Hard PK.
    • Royal Guards are honor-bound to defend the Emperor during an Overthrow and will not be Hard PKed for defending them.
    • You only have one life during an Overthrow.
    • Other factions may not call missions or raids during an Overthrow.
    • The Overthrow attempt will end once either the Emperor or Darth+ who called the Overthrow is defeated.
    • If the Emperor lives, they will decide what happens to the assistants in-character.
    • Once an Overthrow attempt is finished, a one-week grace period begins again.
    • Let a Staff know of the Overthrow attempt so they can watch over and set up spawns.
  • 3a.3 Kaggath:
    • Kaggaths will be set up by staff and only take place if the Emperor gets PKed or abandons the throne.
    • Only Darth+ may claim the throne during a Kaggath. Any Darth+ who claims the throne will be open to Hard PK during the claim. To claim the throne you will type “I claim the throne.” in advert.
    • Any Darth+ can challenge a claim to the throne. Challenging a claim to the throne opens you to Hard PK. To challenge a claim you will type “I challenge (name)’s claim.” in advert.
    • Any rank of sith can assist/defend a claim or challenge but will also be open to Hard PK.
    • Claims only last 10 minutes if nobody challenges the Darth+ claim after 10 minutes, they will become the next Emperor.
    • Challenging a claim will freeze the timer on the claim until one side is defeated.
    • Defeating a claim will start a new timer for your claim.
    • You only have one life during a Kaggath.
    • Other factions may not call missions or raids during a Kaggath.
    • Kaggaths will only end if the throne is filled.
    • If all Darth+ sith either die or refuse to claim during a Kaggath, claims will be open to apprentices and lords as well.
    • Once a Kaggath is finished a one-week grace period begins.
  • 3a.4 Handing The Throne Down:
    • The Emperor may hand down the rank of emperor to any Darth+ sith.
    • The Emperor can not hand down the throne during a grace period.
    • The receiver of the throne will not receive the regular one-week grace period.

Sub-Section 3b: Jedi Guidelines

  • 3b.1 Votes of No Confidence:
    • Any council member may call a vote of no confidence on another council member with valid proof, including the Grandmaster.
    • Votes will be handled in RP by the current council.
    • Any Master+ Jedi are able to call a Vote of No Confidence on the Jedi Council with valid proof. This will start a court hearing for the Vote of No Confidence against the council.
    • The court hearing will be set up by staff but handled in RP.
  • 3b.2 Grandmaster Elections:
    • Master+ Jedi are able to vote on a new grandmaster if the current council is unable to decide. May be turned into a faction-wide vote if necessary.

Sub-Section 3c: Cartel Guidelines

Failure to follow the rules of the Cartel may result in removal of the faction's whitelist.
  • 3c.1 Bounties:
    • You may go onto home planets to complete bounties. This will be treated as a mission with the same cooldown per faction.
    • You can only attack in self-defense or if you have a bounty on the target.
    • You are allowed to help complete other Cartel members' bounties but share a cooldown with them if you do.
    • You may only accept bounties on faction leaders if the pay is equal to or greater than 1 million credits.
  • 3c.2 Conquest and Raids:
    • You are allowed to help a faction in a conquest or raid if you are paid to do so.
    • You only have 1 life in a raid.

Section 4: Perma-Kills and Exile

4.1 Versions of PK

  • Soft PK: Loss of rank in faction and name of character.
  • Hard PK: Loss of rank in faction, name of character, and 35 levels.
  • True PK: Loss of rank in faction, name of character, all levels, and all items.

4.2 PK Duels

  • Challenger must advert the terms of the duel and what version of PK they are dueling for and the receiver must advert if they accept.
  • Staff will watch the PK duel to make sure no foul play is involved.
  • You may PK duel with any version of PK.
  • The duelist may end the fight if mutually agreed upon during the duel.

4.3 Exiles

  • Council+ may exile their faction members if they have a RP reason to do so.
  • Exiles last 30 minutes but can be ended by the Council+ that called it or if the target converts to another faction.
  • While exiled, the exiled are open to Hard PK until the end of the exile.
  • Anyone who attacks an exiled player while they are forgoing the exile is also open to Hard PK until the exiled player dies or the exile ends.

Section 5: Raids and Missions

5.1 Raids

  • Raids must be announced and ended via advert by a Council+.
  • You may have up to 2 lifes in a raid depending on the amount of people on each side. (ex. 15 Sith and 10 Jedi, Sith would only be able to call a 1 life raid.).
  • Faction vaults may only be breached if a raid is ongoing.
  • Raids have a 30 minute cooldown. (Separate from missions.).

5.2 Missions

  • Missions may only be called by Council+.
  • Missions must be announced in @ chat but not advert.
  • PK missions are not a thing.
  • Missions have a 30 minute cooldown. (Separate from raids.).
